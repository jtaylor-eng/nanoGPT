## Hypothesis
e003 showed the trained AS window does not shrink with n, so the target "NoPE support flat in n" was never reached.
Test the target directly, training-free: on the fixed `wstieltjes_q3_d2_xs` checkpoint (lr 1.2e-3, seed 1337),
narrow the NoPE heads' window with position past the train length, window_i = d / s(i),
s(i) = 1 + a ln(max(i, 2048) / 2048) (s = 1 inside the train length, so nothing below 2048 changes). If dispersion of
NoPE heads past 2k costs loss, the a that flattens support lowers ArXiv 16k loss; if the extra keys past 2k carry
useful far context, flattening raises it.

## Prediction (before submission)
Calibration from e001's d sweep at q3 (NoPE support at 16k 34 / 126 / 274 for d 2 / 3 / 4: support ~ d^3) predicts
a = 0.1 (s(16k) = 1.21) brings 16k support back to its 2k value (19.5). Within-model elasticity may differ from the
across-model d sweep.
- NoPE support at 16k decreases monotonically in a (0 > 0.05 > 0.1 > 0.2): 95 %.
- a = 0.1: NoPE support at 16k within +-25 % of the a=0 value at 2k (19.5): 50 %.
Loss = mean per-token loss over query positions 2048..16383 on the 8 papers ("tail loss"; positions < 2048 are
unchanged by construction, and must match a = 0 exactly).
- Some a in {0.05, 0.1, 0.2} lowers tail loss by >= 0.01 vs a = 0: 35 %.
- a = 0.2 raises tail loss vs a = 0: 65 %.
- Loss over positions 0..2047 identical across a (to 1e-4): 97 %.
- Self-check at L=2048 unchanged by the schedule (|wrapped - stock| < 0.002): 99 %.
- Cost (e003 measured 2:14 per 8-paper q3/d2 pass incl. load): 4 passes + smoke <= 0.2 GPU-h: 85 %.

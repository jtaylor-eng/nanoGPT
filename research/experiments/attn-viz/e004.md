## Interpretation
- Forcing NoPE support toward flat in n hurts, monotonically in a, on every paper. Even a = 0.2 (window shrunk
  by 1.42 at 16k) only brings 16k support to 1.23x its 2k value and costs 0.02 nats on positions past 2k. The extra
  keys that enter the window past the train length carry signal, not noise.
- Within one trained model the support's elasticity to the window is ~1 (support 34.4 -> 23.9 for window /1.42),
  not the ~3 implied by e001's across-model d sweep: models trained at larger d also spread their logits less. So a
  per-query window cannot be calibrated post hoc from the d sweep; any n-dependent window has to be trained.
- Together with e003 (the trained AS window learned almost no n-dependence) this argues the "flat support" target
  is the wrong one for windowed Stieltjes at 125M: the fixed map's ~log n support growth is closer to what the model
  wants than a flat support. Caveat: training-free; a model trained with an n-dependent window could re-balance its
  logits. 1 checkpoint, 1 seed, 8 papers.

## Hypothesis
The user-approved follow-up (2026-11-09) is a per-query adaptive window with target "NoPE support flat in n". That
model already exists: AS on windowed Stieltjes is exactly a per-query window d/s_i (substitute lambda = s mu in
`[(lambda - s x_j)^{-q} - d^{-q}]_+`; the s^{-q} factor cancels in the normalisation), and direction `learnable-qd`
e001 trained it at 125M (`aswstieltjes_q3_d2_zi_xs`, lr 1.2e-3 best by val 3.537, ArXiv 16k 4.095 vs fixed q3/d2
3.948: gate failed). What is unknown is the mechanism: did the learned s_i(n) make NoPE support flat in n (and the
loss got worse anyway), or did it not flatten support at all? This experiment measures it with the e001 pipeline,
no training. Hypothesis: the AS scale on NoPE heads grows with position but too slowly to cancel logit drift, so NoPE
support still grows with n, at a rate below fixed q3/d2's x1.76 (2k -> 16k).

## Interpretation
- The existing per-query adaptive window (AS on windowed Stieltjes, window d/s_i) did not learn an n-dependent
  window. Its NoPE scale is ~1.13 at the 2048 train length and moves only a few percent more by 16k, i.e. the
  effective window shrinks from 2/1.13 = 1.77 to ~1.70, while the support still grows 1.73x, the same rate as fixed
  q3/d2 (1.76x). AS here acts as a per-layer constant temperature (5 of 12 layers use it), not as a length knob.
- ASEntmax's scale grows more (1.20 -> 1.33, +11 %) yet its support grows 2.06x: neither AS map flattens support.
  The AS functional form `1 + beta log(pos)^gamma` is trained only on positions < 2048, so nothing pushes it to
  extrapolate the right amount.
- Why the AS row's ArXiv loss is worse (4.171 on these papers vs 3.968 fixed) is not explained by support: its support
  is slightly smaller at every position. More first64 mass at 16k (0.150 vs 0.097) is the one stat that differs
  clearly; learnable-qd e001 already saw a 0.084 gap at ArXiv 2k (domain robustness), so it is not a length effect.
- So "an adaptive window learned at T=2048" is not the mechanism that flattens support. The target itself (does
  flat NoPE support help at 16k?) is untested; e004 tests it training-free.

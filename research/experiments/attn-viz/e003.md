## Hypothesis
The user-approved follow-up (2026-11-09) is a per-query adaptive window with target "NoPE support flat in n". That
model already exists: AS on windowed Stieltjes is exactly a per-query window d/s_i (substitute lambda = s mu in
`[(lambda - s x_j)^{-q} - d^{-q}]_+`; the s^{-q} factor cancels in the normalisation), and direction `learnable-qd`
e001 trained it at 125M (`aswstieltjes_q3_d2_zi_xs`, lr 1.2e-3 best by val 3.537, ArXiv 16k 4.095 vs fixed q3/d2
3.948: gate failed). What is unknown is the mechanism: did the learned s_i(n) make NoPE support flat in n (and the
loss got worse anyway), or did it not flatten support at all? This experiment measures it with the e001 pipeline,
no training. Hypothesis: the AS scale on NoPE heads grows with position but too slowly to cancel logit drift, so NoPE
support still grows with n, at a rate below fixed q3/d2's x1.76 (2k -> 16k).

## Interpretation
- The softmax failure at 16k is dispersion onto a broad mid-to-late floor, not an attention sink. The NoPE softmax
  heads suppress the oldest keys by 4-6 orders of magnitude, so they carry a learned positional bias from the causal
  mask (NoPE heads can infer position); unverified whether the suppressed region tracks the train length 2048.
- The sparse NoPE heads are long-range retrieval heads: few keys, anywhere in the document. Their support grows with
  n because more far keys fall within d of the top, not because they spread onto recent context.
- Caveat: rows are from one paper and 3 heads per row picked for growth (selected for dispersion by construction);
  the group means in e001 are the unbiased summary and agree on both points.

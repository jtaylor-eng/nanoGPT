## Interpretation
- Tail loss as a function of the schedule strength a is a bowl with its minimum at a = 0 (within +-0.001 for
  a in [-0.05, 0]): the fixed q3/d2 model's own support growth past the train length (~log n, 19.5 -> 34.4 from 2k to
  16k) is locally optimal among windows d / (1 + a ln(n / 2048)). Narrowing (toward flat support) and widening
  (toward softmax-like dispersion) both cost loss, symmetric to first order (+0.020 at a = 0.2, +0.018 at -0.2).
- So the support growth in e001 is not a failure to be corrected by a length-dependent window, at least not
  post hoc. The fixed model's long-range deficit vs ASEntmax (ArXiv 16k 3.968 vs 3.946 on these papers) is not a
  window-size problem at inference.
- Limits: training-free, one checkpoint and seed, one schedule family (log n, NoPE heads only, same a for all
  layers). A per-layer schedule could find a small gain where the mean is flat; the e004/e005 slopes (+-0.002 at
  |a| = 0.05..0.1) bound it to a few thousandths of a nat.

## Interpretation
- ALiBi heads are length-invariant in every sparse row (support flat within +-5 % from 512 to 16k); softmax ALiBi k90
  is also flat (~75) once past 2k, though its full support grows. The length story is entirely in the NoPE heads.
- NoPE heads grow their support with position in every map, including windowed Stieltjes. The windowed map is exactly
  sparse at fixed logits, but NoPE logit spread does not stay fixed with n: more keys land within d of the top as n
  grows. Growth 2k -> 16k: softmax k90 x10.0, ASEntmax x2.0, q3/d2 x1.8, q4/d2 x1.8, q3/d3 x1.7, q3/d4 x1.8.
  So the sparse maps differ from softmax by ~5x in growth rate and ~150-270x in level, and from each other mainly in
  level (set by d), not rate. This is consistent with "fixed (q,d) is a frontier": d sets the support size, nothing
  in the fixed map changes its slope with n.
- No attention sink at long range: NoPE first64 mass falls with position for every row (softmax 0.25 -> 0.055).
  The dispersed softmax mass lands on mid/late document keys (local2k 0.16, E[i-j] 7.7k at 16k, about uniform over
  the causal prefix); the sample softmax head L7H6 at 16k puts 1e-7 on the first ~2k keys and a flat 1e-5..1e-4
  floor over the rest. Sparse NoPE heads also look far (local2k 0.18-0.23, E[i-j] 6.7-8.1k): they retrieve a few
  scattered far keys, not recent ones.
- Weak link to the loss: among rows, NoPE k90 at 16k and 16k ArXiv loss are ordered together at the extremes
  (q3/d2 21 -> 3.968; q3/d4 160 -> 4.394; softmax 5846 -> 4.357) but not in the middle (q3/d3 74 and q4/d3 141 both
  ~4.02; ASEntmax 39 is best at 3.946). Support size is not the whole story; 7 checkpoints, 1 seed each.
- Growth is not a few outlier heads: per-layer NoPE mean support for q3/d2 (`summary.json` per_head, [layer][head])
  grows 1.5-2.4x from 2k to 16k in all 12 layers (layer 0: 29 -> 68; layers 1-3: ~8 -> ~14; layers 4-11: 20-28 ->
  31-49). Individual heads still differ (rows plots), so the group mean is a fair summary but not a per-head law.

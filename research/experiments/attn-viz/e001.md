## Hypothesis
On ArXiv papers at 16k, the NoPE heads of the 125M softmax model disperse with query position (entropy keeps
rising through 16k), while the windowed Stieltjes q3/d2 NoPE heads keep an n-independent support and ASEntmax
support grows slowly; i.e. the length-generalisation gap at 16k ArXiv (softmax 4.353, ASEntmax 3.874,
wstieltjes_q3_d2 3.948, all lr 1.2e-3 seed 1337) tracks per-position dispersion of the NoPE heads.

## Prediction (before submission)
Stats are means over 8 papers, 12 layers, the 6 NoPE heads, and the 64 query positions ending at each position.
- softmax_xs NoPE entropy rises monotonically 2k -> 8k -> 16k, with 16k minus 2k >= 0.3 nats: 70 %.
- wstieltjes_q3_d2_xs NoPE support (exact nonzeros) at 16k < 64 keys and <= 1.5x its value at 2k (plateau): 60 %.
- asentmax_zi_xs NoPE support at 16k > 1.2x its value at 2k and < 256 keys: 55 %.
- Ordering of NoPE k90 at 16k: softmax > ASEntmax > windowed Stieltjes: 80 %.
- (e003 preview, recorded now) softmax NoPE first64 mass at 16k >= 0.2 (sink): 50 %.
- Sweep rows (q3/d3, q3/d4, q4/d2, q4/d3): NoPE support at 16k grows with d at fixed q (d4 > d3 > d2): 75 %.
- Cost (measured, smoke job 8367344, 1 paper at 16k per row on nextgen A100 40 GB): 8 s/paper softmax, 47 s
  ASEntmax, 11 s windowed q3/d2, ~25 s load + check per task. 7 rows x 8 papers ~ 21 min ~ 0.35 GPU-h;
  prediction: total <= 0.5 GPU-h: 85 %.

Honesty note: the predictions above the cost lines were written before the smoke; the smoke printed paper-0
numbers for the three headline rows (softmax NoPE entropy 5.35 -> 8.02 nats 2k -> 16k; ASEntmax NoPE support
44 -> 94; windowed q3/d2 NoPE support 20 -> 36). The predictions were not edited after seeing them; the sweep-row
and cost predictions were added after.

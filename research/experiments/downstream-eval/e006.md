## Interpretation
The seed-1337 spread that broke prediction 1 is mostly seed noise, not a map effect: the between-seed SD on LAMBADA
accuracy (0.62 softmax, 0.94 wStieltjes q3/d2, 1.63 ASEntmax) is 1-3x the per-run binomial SE (0.58), and ASEntmax's
three seeds alone span 2.9 pt (22.16-25.05), the same size as the four-row seed-1337 spread. On 3-seed means the order
is ASEntmax 24.04 > softmax 22.92 > wStieltjes q3/d2 22.53; ASEntmax minus softmax is +1.1 pt with Welch t ~1.1
(n = 3 each), not significant. Among lr 1.2e-3 runs LAMBADA accuracy is uncorrelated with val loss (r = -0.10,
n = 10), so seed-level accuracy is noise around a near-common level; across all 16 runs r = -0.55, carried by LR
(every 6e-4 run is 1.0-2.2 pt below its row's 1.2e-3 3-seed mean, as in e001). HellaSwag separates nothing (28.7-29.8 %).
LAMBADA perplexity is the one place a map pattern is suggestive: ASEntmax 3-seed mean 88.3 vs softmax 104.2 and
wStieltjes 101.8 (Welch t ~3.1 vs softmax, df ~3; 2 of 3 ASEntmax seeds at 83-85 while no softmax or wStieltjes seed
is below 97.9), and the one AS-wStieltjes run (88.5) sits with the ASEntmax seeds. Read that as "adaptive scale may
help last-word prediction", flagged hypothesis only: softmax at lr 2.4e-3 also reaches 87.3, so an effective-LR
confound is not excluded, and AS-wStieltjes has one seed.
Versus e001 (RNG-replay checkpoints, seed 1337): softmax 22.98 -> 22.61, ASEntmax 23.23 -> 25.05, wStieltjes q3/d2
22.94 -> 21.64. The e001 three-row agreement within 0.3 pt was luck on one seed; the clean 3-seed data say zero-shot
LAMBADA at 125M has a ~1-1.6 pt seed SD and cannot rank the maps without more seeds.

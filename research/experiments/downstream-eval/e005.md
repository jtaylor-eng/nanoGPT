## Interpretation
1. At 125M / 2.5B tokens single-needle retrieval is weak even at the train length (max 46.5 % at 2k) and near the
   floor past it (every row <= 6 % at 16k). The task is informative (backlog item 4 does not trigger) but the
   e006 gate (any row > 50 % at 16k) fails by a wide margin, so distractor needles would measure noise.
2. Past the train length the sparse maps keep a few points where softmax has none: softmax is 0.5-1 % at 4k-16k at
   both LRs and its few hits are needles next to the question; ASEntmax and q3/d2 retain early-context needles.
   The answer log-prob mirrors the ArXiv/PG-19 loss story: softmax collapses (-5.6 -> -19.1), ASEntmax and d = 2
   Stieltjes stay within ~3 nats of their 2k value.
3. The between-map ordering is not stable under LR: ASEntmax is best at lr 6e-4 (25.5 / 12.5 / 6.0) and q3/d2 is
   best at lr 1.2e-3 (13.5 / 6.5 / 5.0), while q3/d2 lr 6e-4 and softmax lr 6e-4 are near zero even at 2k (6.5 and
   4.5 %). Within-row LR swings (up to 25 pt at 2k) exceed between-row gaps, with one seed per cell. So this does
   not rank ASEntmax vs windowed Stieltjes; it only separates "sparse" from "softmax" past 2k, by a few points.
4. Among Stieltjes cells, d = 2 is what retains anything past 2k (q3/d2, q4/d2 at 4k: 13.5, 11.0); d = 3, 4 fall
   to 0 at 8k, matching their steeper answer log-prob curves. Consistent with the 125M finding that d = 2 is the
   long-range end of the frontier.
Alternative explanations not ruled out: retrieval at 2k may be limited by the needle format (5-digit number in BPE
pieces) rather than attention; the depth profile (early needles survive) may reflect NAPE NoPE heads anchoring on
the context start rather than retrieval proper.

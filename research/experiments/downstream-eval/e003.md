## Interpretation
Failure cause: stage 2 forced the canonical BPE of the bare number ("74862" -> "748"+"62"), but after "\n" the models
copy the digit pieces of the needle, which was tokenized with a leading space (" 74862" -> " 7"+"48"+"62", so "7"+"48"+"62").
The failures are exactly the numbers whose two tokenizations differ (74862, 52260, 59301, 76779, 35465); the
agreements (73848, 81622, 23755) are numbers whose spaced and bare splits coincide. q3/d2 agreed 20/20 because it
answers " NNNNN" (space form) when it is right. This is a different cause from e002 (wrong answer prefix), but the same
class: a teacher-forced chain cannot equal greedy decoding when the model may emit any tokenization.
Side evidence (n=20, anecdotal): the windowed Stieltjes row keeps the answer log-prob nearly flat to 16k (-3.6 -> -6.4)
while softmax falls off a cliff past train length (-4.9 -> -18.8) and ASEntmax sits in between (-5.2 -> -10.0).

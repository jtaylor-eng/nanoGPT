## Change
New `eval_niah.py` (RULER S-NIAH-1 style passkey). Each sample is exactly L tokens: filler = first tokens of PG-19
eval doc (i mod 128); needle "\nThe special magic number is NNNNN.\n" (5-digit, uniform) inserted at depth
U(bin/10, (bin+1)/10) snapped forward to the next sentence end; question "\n\nWhat is the special magic number
mentioned in the text above? The special magic number is"; answer " NNNNN". 300 samples per length (30 per depth
bin, SE <= 2.9 pt), identical samples for every checkpoint (seeded by (1234, L)).
Scoring: one teacher-forced forward; correct = argmax equals every answer token and the argmax after the answer is
not digit-led. This equals "greedy decoding emits the number and stops it", i.e. greedy exact match of the
immediate continuation, at 1 forward instead of up to 8 (deviation from the backlog's 8-token greedy decode, chosen
for cost: 8 recomputed forwards at 16k cost ~1.25 GPU-h per Stieltjes checkpoint, over the 5 GPU-h ceiling for ten).
A greedy-decode cross-check (8 tokens, 20 samples at 2048) is stored in `gen_check`. Also logs the answer
log-prob and first-token accuracy as continuous scores.
`osc/eval_niah.sbatch`: array 0-9, nextgen, 1:00 walltime. Output `$LM_RESULTS/125m/<row>/lr<lr>/niah.json`.
Cost from the PG-19 eval forward throughput (s per 16k sequence: softmax 0.86, ASEntmax 1.16, Stieltjes 2.13;
8k 0.15/0.30/0.54): Stieltjes ~0.24 h, ASEntmax ~0.13 h, softmax ~0.09 h per checkpoint, ~2 GPU-h for all ten.

## Hypothesis
User extension 2026-11-15, item (2). Adaptive-temperature softmax (arXiv 2410.01104) trained in the loop on the NoPE heads
(ALiBi heads plain softmax): each query row's logits are multiplied by beta(H) = where(H > 0.5, max(poly(H), 1), 1), H = the
Shannon entropy of the row's plain softmax. If an entropy-dependent sharpening is enough to hold dispersion off beyond the
train length, adatemp improves on softmax at ArXiv / PG-19 16k; the polynomial was fitted on <= 16 items and returns beta = 1
above H = 5.95 nats, so very diffuse rows at 16k get no correction.

## What was read (paper, local LaTeX `context/adaptive_temp_softmax_latex/`)
- Sec. 4 and the JAX listing (label fig:jax): two softmaxes, `poly_fit = [-0.037, 0.481, -2.3, 4.917, -1.791]` through
  `jnp.polyval`, entropy with `log(p + 1e-9)`, `beta = where(entropy > 0.5, maximum(polyval, 1.0), 1.0)`,
  `softmax(logits * beta)`. No stop_gradient in the listing, so the gradient flows through beta; implemented that way.
- The CLRS-Text paragraph: for Gemma 2B the function is used as-is, the same polynomial, in every head, during fine-tuning
  and at inference. This run trains from scratch and restricts it to the NoPE heads (user instruction); both are departures
  from the paper's setup. I did not find a from-scratch LM result in the sections read (Sec. 4, 5, App. on streaming).
- App. "streamed" algorithm: entropy from running sums inside a flash kernel, O(n) memory. Not implemented as a kernel here.
- Evaluated by hand: beta > 1 only for 0.87 < H < 5.95 nats, peak 2.39 at H = 4.1; beta is continuous at both ends.

## Interpretation
Adaptive-temperature softmax trained in the loop on the NoPE heads is softmax at 125M: tie at the train length (+0.003,
paired), ArXiv 16k 4.231 inside the softmax seed range (4.215-4.283), 6 % of the softmax-to-sparse gap closed against
SSMax's ~45 %. It costs 2.4x softmax per step in this implementation and buys nothing measurable.
- The mechanism behaves as the pre-registered reasoning said. beta > 1 only for 0.87 < H < 5.95 nats, and the softmax NoPE
  heads sit at 7.7 nats in the last quarter of a 16k context, where beta = 1. The head statistics add a second effect: the
  trained model is sharper than softmax at 512 (entropy 4.06 vs 4.54) and more diffuse at 16k (7.94 vs 7.66; FineWeb 8.31
  vs 7.72), i.e. training leans on beta for sharpness inside the fitted range and loses it past the range. My reading; the
  pre-beta entropy was not logged, so this is inferred from the post-beta maps, not measured.
- The small paired gains on long documents (ArXiv -0.02 to -0.04 at 4k-16k, PG-19 -0.01 to -0.02 at 8k-16k) have a
  consistent sign and grow with length, but they are smaller than the softmax seed range (0.068 at ArXiv 16k), so one seed
  does not establish them. The FineWeb cost (+0.004 at every length up to 4k) is equally small and equally consistent.
- With e009 this orders the two softmax-family references: a length-dependent temperature (SSMax, log n) recovers about
  half of the sparse rows' long-document advantage; an entropy-dependent temperature fitted on <= 16 items recovers none
  at 16k. Neither changes the train-length tie. The sparse rows (ASEntmax, windowed Stieltjes, AS-windowed) remain 0.33-0.38
  below SSMax and 0.58-0.63 below adatemp at ArXiv 16k (seed 1337).
Caveats: one seed, one LR; NoPE heads only and trained from scratch (the paper fine-tunes with the function in every head);
the paper's polynomial unchanged. A polynomial refitted for n up to 16k would be a different method and was not tried.

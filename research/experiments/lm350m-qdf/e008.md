## Interpretation
Neither variant changes the picture, and one is ruled out.
- d = 1.5 is strictly worse than d = 2 at 125M (train length and every long eval, mostly outside the seed range). The window
  hypothesis for the FineWeb 16k uptick is refuted: narrowing the window makes the uptick bigger. With e007 (higher LR also
  narrows the support and also hurts long documents) the direction at 125M is consistently "narrower is worse"; the untested
  side is wider (q3/d3 was the replayed stage's alternative).
- Adaptive scale on the Stieltjes window buys a small long-document gain (ArXiv 16k -0.033, PG-19 16k -0.036 vs the same-seed
  fixed window; ArXiv outside the fixed-window seed range, PG-19 at its edge) for +0.014 at train length (outside the
  fixed-window seed range, but under the +-0.05 single-run final-eval bounce quoted for Stieltjes rows). Single seed, and e006
  showed how misleading seed 1337 alone was for the AS rows (ASEntmax 16k ranges 0.04-0.23), so this is a hint, not a result.
  It does not "close the gap to ASEntmax" because e006 shows there is no gap to close: fixed-window q3/d2, AS-windowed q3/d2
  and ASEntmax all sit inside one another's seed ranges at 125M.
For 350M the simplest Stieltjes row (fixed-window q3/d2) remains the right representative: it ties on every metric, has the
smallest seed spread, and needs no zero-init scale; aswstieltjes would need seeds before it could replace it.

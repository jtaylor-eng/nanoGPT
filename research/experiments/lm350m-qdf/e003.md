**The 350M model is worse than the 125M model on every eval**, with a train-minus-val gap of -0.50 (6e-4) / -0.32
(3e-4) at what was meant to be 1 epoch. Periodic val is flat at ~3.45 from iter 2500 while train keeps falling.

**Cause: data replay on every resume (bug, affects every chained run).** `train.py:124` seeds `torch.manual_seed(1337)`
at process start and the checkpoint (`save_ckpt`, `train.py:~280`) does not store the RNG state, so every resumed
segment redraws the same `torch.randint` window starts as segment 1 (upstream nanoGPT behaviour; harmless there because
upstream runs are not chained). Verified by CPU simulation of the exact call sequence (first batch, eval draws every 250
iters, 32 micro-batches/iter, len(train.bin) = 2.5e9): 99.1 % of segment 2's and 99.5 % of segment 3's training windows
are windows of segment 1 (`/tmp/rngsim/sim.py`, 212k windows per segment). Checked and ruled out: shard overlap
(fineweb25B = kjj0 shards 11-35, fineweb10B = 1-10; 140 sampled 64 KB chunks, no duplicates) and data-path errors
(guard log). So the unique training data of a run is one segment, repeated (iters per segment from run.logs / e001):

| size | row | iters / segment | unique tokens | repeats over 4770 iters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 125M | softmax_xs | 2174 | 1.14B | 2.2x |
| 125M | asentmax_zi_xs | ~1360 | ~0.71B | ~3.5x |
| 125M | wstieltjes_q3_d2_xs | ~1000 | ~0.52B | ~4.7x |

## Interpretation
Partial (2 of 8 surviving cells): the 350M numbers measure memorisation of a 0.2-0.44B-token subset repeated 6-12x,
not scaling. They cannot answer the direction's question, and the 125M "crossover" (e004 and the original 125M stage)
is confounded the same way. The fix (store and restore the CPU/CUDA RNG state in ckpt.pt, or reseed with
`seed + iter_num` on resume) is small but changes the protocol mid-stage, so it is the user's call.

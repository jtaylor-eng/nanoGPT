## Interpretation
The 125M -> 350M slowdown is 2.6x (softmax), 2.5x (ASEntmax), 2.6x (Stieltjes): close to the FLOP ratio, so the
sparse kernels do not scale worse than softmax. Micro-batch 8 is the best fit for every row (ASEntmax gains 6 %
over bs4, Stieltjes 2 %), which is the config default (bs8 x accum 32 = 256 sequences = 0.5M tokens), so the
stage needs no per-row micro-batch override. The full planned stage fits the 500 GPU-h budget at 2.5B tokens
with ~190 GPU-h to spare, so the user's "does not fit" decision (a/b/c) is not triggered.

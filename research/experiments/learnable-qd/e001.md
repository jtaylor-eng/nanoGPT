## Interpretation
The adaptive scale does not help windowed Stieltjes at 125M; it hurts out of domain. The deficit is not a
length effect: ArXiv is already 0.084 worse at 2k (the training length) and the 2k -> 16k improvement is smaller
(-0.130 vs -0.193 for fixed q3/d2; ASEntmax -0.163). In-domain FineWeb is unchanged at 2k and slightly worse at 16k.
So the per-query scale on top of an n-independent window buys nothing on FineWeb and costs domain robustness
(ArXiv) — plausibly because s scales the window (d/s) per query from content features that are FineWeb-tuned
(an interpretation, not checked: the learned scale was not inspected). This matches the 51M result (AS on windowed
Stieltjes neutral) with a worse sign at 125M. Under the proposal's first-order equivalence (scale s = window d/s),
per-query window control realised as a temperature is not what separates ASEntmax from windowed Stieltjes at
this size; the heavy tail / map shape remains the candidate deficit. Single seed; the 0.147 ArXiv 16k gap is
~3x the +-0.05 eval bounce, so the sign is solid, the magnitude less so.

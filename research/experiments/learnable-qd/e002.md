## Interpretation
Per-head learnable (q_h, d_h) is a clear negative at 125M, and worse than the hypothesis allowed: it is 0.085
behind the fixed q3/d2 cell it was initialised from on val@2048 and 0.375 behind on ArXiv 16k (single seed; both
gaps exceed the +-0.05 eval bounce, the val gap by ~1.5x, the ArXiv gap by ~7x). It is behind on ArXiv already
at 2k (4.398 vs 4.141), so like e001 this is a domain-robustness cost, not a length effect; ArXiv barely
improves from 2k to 16k (-0.075 vs -0.193 for fixed q3/d2).
- It is not explained by where (q, d) ended up. At lr 6e-4 the learned values (q ~3.16, d ~2.48) sit between
  the fixed q3/d2 and q3/d3 cells, yet that run is 0.105 behind fixed q3/d2 at the same LR on val (3.646 vs 3.541)
  and 0.248 on ArXiv 16k. Every fixed cell between q3-4 and d2-4 lands within 3.49-3.53 val. So the cost comes
  from learning (q, d) during training (a moving map), or from the larger, heterogeneous per-head spread, not
  from the endpoint values. Not separated here (unverified which).
- The learned values do not converge: the plateau level scales with the LR (d ~2.2 / 2.5 / 3.2), consistent with
  Adam steps of size ~lr on theta and a weak, noisy gradient; the 125M gradient probe's "wants d wider" sign
  holds at every LR.
- Pattern shared with e001: both added adaptivity (per-query scale, per-head q/d) lowers train-split loss and
  raises val and ArXiv loss, with the train/val gap ordered softmax < ASEntmax < fixed Stieltjes < AS-Stieltjes
  < learnable q/d. With a ~1-epoch dataset this reads as extra adaptivity in the windowed map being spent on
  fitting seen FineWeb text. A hypothesis, not tested.
- Fused learnable-q/d kernel: correct (qdtest2) but 43 % slower than the integer-q chain (20.0 vs 14.0 s/step).

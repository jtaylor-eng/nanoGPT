## Interpretation
Position-dependent temperature alone buys part of the long-context gain and costs nothing at train length: SSMax closes 0.261 of
the 0.663 softmax-to-ASEntmax gap at ArXiv 16k (39 %), improves ArXiv at every length including 2k (-0.110, where it also beats
ASEntmax by 0.031) and PG-19 slightly, with val@2048 equal to softmax (-0.003, inside the +-0.05 bounce). But its ArXiv curve still
rises from 2k to 16k (+0.168, like softmax's +0.319, unlike ASEntmax's -0.265), and the remaining 0.402 gap to ASEntmax at 16k is
far outside the bounce. So the 'falling with length' signature of the sparse rows is not reproduced by a fixed log-n temperature:
the residual is sparsity and/or a per-query (content-dependent) scale, which this design does not separate (ASEntmax has both).
Single seed, single LR; the SSMax scale init is the asentmax `nakanishi` port, not tuned. Backlog item 6 (SSMax at lr 6e-4) is not
triggered (no divergence, val@2048 not behind softmax).

## Interpretation
All-NoPE softmax is a cliff, not a continuation of the trend: it is fine up to the train length (ArXiv 2k 4.053, equal to 6/6) and
blows up as soon as it leaves it (+1.0 nats on FineWeb at 4k, ~+3.5-4 nats at 16k on every set). The 6/6 -> 4/8 -> 2/10 ArXiv gain
for softmax therefore depends on keeping at least two ALiBi heads; the ALiBi heads are what keeps NoPE softmax stable beyond 2048,
and with zero of them softmax has no length generalisation at all. It also costs 0.13 nats at the train length versus 6/6, so the
locality bias helps in-distribution too. Combined with e003/e004 (Stieltjes and ASEntmax ArXiv 16k curves fall with length at every
split, ASEntmax flat across splits), the direction's answer to "does it live on the NoPE heads?" is no: the sparse/adaptive maps'
extrapolation is a property of the map, the split mainly moves softmax, and softmax needs ALiBi heads to avoid collapse. Single seed,
but the a0 effect is ~70x the +-0.05 bounce.

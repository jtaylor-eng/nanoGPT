## Interpretation
Moving heads from ALiBi to NoPE does not change ASEntmax's long-range ArXiv loss (3.707 / 3.722 / 3.745, a 0.04 drift inside the
bounce) and costs nothing at train length (val@2048 spread 0.02). Its ArXiv curve falls 2k -> 16k at every split (-0.265, -0.242,
-0.249). The NoPE-head cost on FineWeb 16k (+0.166) and PG-19 16k (+0.058) appears in every map (softmax +0.398 FW 16k), roughly
halved for ASEntmax. Combined with e003 (Stieltjes gap 0.389 / 0.536 / 0.335, softmax ArXiv 16k improving 4.370 -> 4.164):
the head split moves softmax, not the sparse maps; the sparse maps' extrapolation does not live on "more NoPE heads". Their
advantage over softmax at ArXiv 16k shrinks at 2/10 only because softmax improves. ASEntmax does not follow Stieltjes' direction
(Stieltjes a4 improved 0.17, ASEntmax a4 +0.015), so the "e004 follows Stieltjes" backlog claim is a miss as well.
Single seed: the only effect here clearly outside the bounce is the FW 16k rise at 2/10.
